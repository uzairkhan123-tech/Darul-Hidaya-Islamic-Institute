About the Tutor

Meet Your Teacher

Maulana Muhammad Umar Farooq
Islamic Scholar · Expert Quran Teacher

Maulana Muhammad Umar Farooq is a qualified Islamic scholar and dedicated Quran teacher who has spent the past decade guiding students of every age and background through the correct recitation, memorisation and understanding of the Holy Quran. His teaching is rooted in authentic Tajweed rules and the traditional method passed down by scholars, combined with a patient, modern and student-friendly approach.

Over ten years he has taught hundreds of students from across Pakistan, the Gulf, Europe and North America — from young children taking their very first Noorani Qaida lesson, to adults completing Hifz and learning Tafseer. Every student receives his personal attention and a clear, structured plan.

Our Courses

Programs for Every Step of the Journey

From the very first Arabic letter to the memorisation of the entire Quran — our courses are structured so that every student finds the right starting point and grows step by step with proper Tajweed.

ا

Noorani Qaida

The foundation course. Students learn Arabic letters, their sounds, joining of letters and basic pronunciation rules — the essential first step before reciting the Quran.

Beginners · Ages 4+
📖

Nazira Quran

Recitation of the Holy Quran by looking. Students read directly from the Mushaf with correct pronunciation and Tajweed, building fluency and confidence over the entire Quran.

After Qaida
۞

Hifz ul Quran

Memorisation of the Holy Quran under a structured revision plan. Daily new lesson, recent revision and old revision cycles ensure the Quran is retained for life, inshaAllah.

Dedicated Students
✦

Tajweed Course

The rules of correct Quranic recitation — Makharij (points of articulation), Sifaat (qualities of letters), Ghunnah, Madd and more. Recite the Quran the way it was revealed.

All Levels
🕮

Tafseer ul Quran

Understanding the meaning of the Holy Quran — the context of verses, reasons of revelation and the lessons for daily life, taught in a clear and authentic manner.

Advanced
🕌

Islamic Studies

Daily Duas, proper Salah, Aqeedah, Seerah of the Prophet ﷺ, Fiqh and good character (Akhlaq). A complete Islamic upbringing alongside Quran lessons.

All Ages
